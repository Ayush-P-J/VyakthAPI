Connect a customer's Tally once, then use their ledgers, vouchers and stock through REST and webhooks. You don't write XML or TDL, and you don't build a connector for each project.

<Callout type="warn" title="Not yet proven on a real TallyPrime">
  Everything so far has been tested against a simulated Tally. Don't post to a customer's live books until this notice is removed.
</Callout>

## How it fits together

- **Connector.** A Windows program on your customer's PC. It talks to Tally there and keeps a secure channel open to us.
- **Connect link.** You send this to your customer. They choose a company and decide what to share.
- **Connection.** One Tally company your project can reach. Every data call names a connection.
- **Synced and live reads.** Synced reads come from our copy and work when the customer's PC is off. Live reads ask Tally directly, so Tally must be open.
- **Write jobs.** A write is queued and runs once on the connector. Your app hears the result from the job or a webhook.

## Base address

Calls go to `/v1` on your API address, with a key from the console:

```sh
curl https://api.example.com/v1/connections \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $KEY"
```
