title: Quickstart
description: From a new account to your first ledger and your first write.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a key

In the console, open **API keys** and choose **Create API key**. Choose **Full access** for this walkthrough, then copy the key. It's shown once.

```sh
export API=https://api.example.com
export KEY=hx_live_...
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect a company

Open **Connections**, choose **Connect a customer**, and send the link to your customer. See [Connect a customer](/docs/connect-a-customer) for what they'll see.

Once they've finished, list your connections and keep the `id`:

```sh
curl $API/v1/connections -H "authorization: Bearer $KEY"
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Read ledgers

```sh
export ID=<connection id>
curl "$API/v1/connections/$ID/ledgers?limit=10" -H "authorization: Bearer $KEY"
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Post a receipt

Amounts are in paise, and debits must equal credits. The `idempotency-key` makes the request safe to retry.

```sh
curl -X POST $API/v1/connections/$ID/receipts \
  -H "authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -H "idempotency-key: order-1042" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"date":"2026-11-03","entries":[
    {"ledger":"HDFC Bank","side":"Dr","amountPaise":25000000},
    {"ledger":"A-1203 Customer","side":"Cr","amountPaise":25000000}]}'
```

You get `202` and a job. Read its result from `/v1/write-jobs/{id}`, or see it under **Write jobs** in the console.

</Step>
</Steps>
